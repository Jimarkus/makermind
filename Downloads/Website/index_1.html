Frequently asked

Anything not covered here, just ask — we reply within one business day.

How long is each session?

Sixty minutes by default. Ninety-minute sessions are available for team work, CAD projects or exam preparation — just ask when you book.

Do you tutor online or in person?

Both. Online sessions run over video with screen share and live CAD, which works well for code and design reviews. In-person tutoring is available across Canberra.

What year levels do you work with?

Years 7 to 12 for coursework and competition coaching, plus FLL students from around Year 4. We also help first-year university students with introductory engineering and programming.

Does my child need to be on a competition team?

Not at all. About half our students come purely for physics, engineering or programming coursework, with no competition involved.

What if we need to cancel a session?

Reschedule free with at least 12 hours notice. Inside 12 hours the session is charged, though we will always try to find another time first.

Can you work with our whole team?

Yes. Team coaching is quoted per season based on team size and how often you want us on site. Most teams book weekly sessions through build season plus competition-day support.
